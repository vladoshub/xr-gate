Controller Gestures

Gestures work the same way on either hand. Menu requires both hands.

Ready pose: ring finger bent, index and middle fingers straight. Keep your pinky comfortable.

Finger Map

The open palm below identifies each finger. Use the ready pose above when controlling movement and buttons.

1Thumb → Movement + A/B
Move your thumb to steer. A thumb–index circle selects A or B with the finger shapes shown below.
2Index → Trigger
Bend to press; straighten to release.
3Middle → Grip
Bend to press; straighten to release.
4Ring → Part of stick click
From the ready pose, tuck your thumb, pause, then straighten your ring finger. Follow the two steps under Thumbstick Click.
5Pinky → No separate button
Ignored for the ready pose and stick click. Follow the finger shapes for A and B.

Buttons

ButtonGesture
TriggerBend your index finger. Straighten it to release.
GripBend your middle finger. Straighten it to release.
AMake a circle with your thumb and index finger; keep the other three fingers straight.
BMake a circle with your thumb and index finger; bend the other three fingers.
Thumbstick click / touchpad pressThree straight fingers with your thumb tucked — follow the steps below.
MenuBriefly hold both hands open with your thumbs away from your palms.

For Trigger and Grip, try bending the finger only partway. A full curl may not be necessary.

Thumb Movement

Keep your wrist still and your thumb away from your palm.

MovementThumb gesture
ForwardStraighten and slightly arch your thumb backward.
BackwardBend the tip toward your palm while keeping the base away from it.
Left / rightPoint your thumb in the desired direction.
DiagonalCombine sideways movement with bending or straightening.
StopReturn your thumb to neutral or tuck it against your palm.

Backward movement requires a more deliberate gesture.

In wrist control mode, keep your thumb out and tilt your wrist in the desired direction. Diagonals also work. Tucking your thumb stops movement.

Thumbstick Click

  1. Keep your index and middle fingers straight and your ring finger bent. Tuck your whole thumb against your palm, keep the thumb and index tips apart, and pause briefly.
  2. Keep your thumb tucked and straighten your ring finger. The three straight fingers activate the click. Your pinky is ignored.

Bend your ring finger to release. Tucking your thumb to stop movement does not activate the click by itself.

Trigger, Grip, and movement can work together. After A, B, or a thumbstick click, release the gesture and return to the ready pose.
