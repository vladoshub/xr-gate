Controller gestures

Select the matching mode in the launcher’s Controllers list. Simple gestures are the default.

Simple gestures

No calibration is needed. Pinch and palm Grip also work during an active touchpad session; thumbs-up with a fist clicks the panel instead of B/Y, and V exits instead of X/A.

ButtonGesture
TriggerPinch your thumb and index fingertips together. Separate them to release.
GripOpen your palm toward yourself, with all five fingers straight and spread.
Grip + TriggerClose your hand into a fist, with the thumb folded against the fingers. This works outside an active touchpad.
X (left) / A (right)Outside the touchpad, show V: spread your straight index and middle fingers; bend ring and pinky. Fingers point up, palm away from you. An angled wrist does not press the button.
Y (left) / B (right)Curl index, middle, ring and pinky into a fist. Keep the thumb away from the palm and point its main phalanx straight up. Its tip may be straight or bent; no backward arch is required.
MenuStraighten only the index finger on each hand. Point the two index fingers toward each other, with both hands visible.

Virtual touchpad

Enabled by default, independently for each hand. Fully straighten all five fingers and separate thumb and index. Point the open palm away from your head, like a stop sign: you see the back of your hand, with the fingers unfolded at the knuckles. Keep this pose briefly to activate. Its position relative to your head then becomes the centre. Head movement and rotation are accounted for continuously.

ActionGesture
Move on the panelMove the hand left/right or up/down relative to your head. Diagonals work. The edge is 10 cm from the centre; movement beyond it stays at the edge.
PressAfter activating the panel, curl index, middle, ring and pinky into a fist. Lift the thumb away from the palm and point it straight up. Its tip may be straight or bent. Keep moving the hand to change direction while holding the click. Release the pose to release the click. This sends no B/Y. A fist with a tucked thumb does not click.
ReleaseOpen the palm away again. The centre stays in place.
Exit / set a new centreShow V: spread your straight index and middle fingers; bend ring and pinky. Point the fingers up, palm away from you. An angled V does not exit. This exits without pressing X/A; release V before using it as a normal button. Loss of hand or head tracking also exits. Wrist rotation alone does not exit, even past 35 degrees. The next activation captures a new centre.

While the panel is active, pinch sends Trigger, an open palm toward yourself sends Grip, and thumbs-up with a fist clicks the panel without B/Y. These gestures keep the panel axes working. V exits without X/A. No replacement gesture is assigned to X/A or B/Y inside the panel. The other hand remains independent.

To disable only the panel on Linux, add SIMPLE_GESTURES_TOUCHPAD=0 to the launch command. In the Windows launcher’s .env, set XR_SIMPLE_GESTURES_TOUCHPAD=0. Restart tracking after changing it.

Default radius: 10 cm. Simple mode adds 30 degrees of outward yaw to the virtual hands and controllers. Set SIMPLE_GESTURES_TOUCHPAD_RADIUS_M and SIMPLE_GESTURES_POSE_YAW_OFFSET_DEGREES in the launch script (0 disables extra yaw). In the Windows .env, prefix these names with XR_. The yaw is skipped while a side uses controller IMU orientation and returns with optical hands.

On Linux, stop tracking and restart with:

SIMPLE_GESTURES=1 ./run_xr_client.sh --config xreal_ultra

Ready pose

Keep your index and middle fingers straight, ring and pinky bent, and thumb relaxed near the palm. Turn your hands inward toward your body so the cameras can see them well. In VR, the hands/controllers are rotated forward by the configured offset.

Buttons

ButtonGesture
TriggerBend your index finger; straighten to release.
GripBend your middle finger; straighten to release.
X (left) / A (right)Make a thumb–index circle; keep the other three fingers straight.
Y (left) / B (right)Make a thumb–index circle; bend the other three fingers.
Thumbstick click / touchpad pressOpen one palm toward yourself, with all five fingers straight and spread. Change the pose to release.
MenuHold both hands open, palms away from you, like a stop sign. You should see the backs of your hands.

For Trigger and Grip, try bending the finger only partway. A full curl may not be necessary.

Left hand: X/Y. Right hand: A/B. Trigger, Grip and D-pad can work together. The open-palm touchpad gesture pauses movement, Trigger and Grip.

Thumb movement

Keep your wrist still and your thumb away from your palm.

MovementThumb gesture
NeutralKeep the thumb relaxed and slightly away from the palm; no movement.
ForwardStraighten and slightly arch the thumb away from the palm.
BackwardClearly curl the tip toward the palm while keeping the base away.
Left / rightMove the thumb in the desired direction.
DiagonalCombine sideways movement with bending or straightening.
D-pad offPress the thumb against the palm.

If wrist D-pad is selected in your profile, keep your thumb out and tilt the wrist in the desired direction. Diagonals work too. Tuck the thumb to stop. Wrist and thumb modes are alternatives.
