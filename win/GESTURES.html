Controller gestures

Choose the same mode in the launcher’s Controllers list. Controller emulation is the default. Keep your hands visible to the glasses’ cameras.

Start here

Neutral / ready position

Keep the index and middle fingers straight, ring finger and pinky bent, and thumb relaxed. Turn your wrists inward toward your body so the cameras see the fingers clearly. The configured yaw offset turns the virtual controllers forward in VR.

Both hands

Trigger

Bend your index finger. Straighten it to release. You can try a partial bend.

Both hands

Grip

Bend your middle finger. Straighten it to release. You can try a partial bend.

Face button

X / A

Touch thumb and index fingertips to make a circle. Keep the other three fingers straight. Left: X. Right: A.

Face button

Y / B

Make the same thumb–index circle and bend the other three fingers. Left: Y. Right: B.

Palm toward you

Touchpad / thumbstick click

Open one palm toward yourself, with all five fingers straight and spread. You see your palm. Return to the ready position to release. Movement, Trigger and Grip pause while showing this separate gesture.

Both hands together

Menu

Hold both hands open, palms facing away from you, like a stop sign. You see the backs of your hands. Hold briefly; both hands must remain visible.

Movement with your thumb

Keep your wrist still. These drawings show the thumb poses; the other fingers stay in the ready position. Trigger and Grip can work together with movement. Diagonals combine sideways movement with forward or backward.

Thumb D-pad

Neutral — no movement

Keep your thumb relaxed and slightly away from your palm.

Thumb D-pad

Forward

Straighten and slightly arch your thumb away from your palm.

Thumb D-pad

Backward

Curl the tip toward your palm while keeping the base away. Make the bend clear.

Thumb D-pad

Left

Sweep your thumb to the left without turning your wrist.

Thumb D-pad

Right

Sweep your thumb to the right without turning your wrist.

Thumb D-pad

D-pad off

Press your thumb against your palm to stop movement.

If wrist D-pad is selected in your profile, keep your thumb out and tilt your wrist in the direction you want to move. Tuck the thumb to stop. Thumb and wrist modes are alternatives.

The original hand gestures use the same mapping on either hand: pinch for Trigger and grab for Grip. Open your hand to release. There is no thumb D-pad or open-palm touchpad gesture in this mode.

Both hands

Pinch → Trigger

Bring your thumb and index fingertips together. Separate them to release.

Both hands

Grab → Grip

Bend the middle, ring and little fingers to grab. Open them to release.

Released

Open hand → release

Relax and open your hand to release the grab and pinch.

Additional legacy bindings

If enabled in the runtime profile, thumbs-up and pointing provide additional A/B inputs. These shape gestures can overlap with Grip; application bindings determine their action.

Profile-dependent

Thumbs-up → A input

Make a fist and extend your thumb.

Profile-dependent

Point → B input

Straighten only your index finger; bend the other three.
